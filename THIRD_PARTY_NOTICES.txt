npm-shrinkwrap.json records the resolved packages, exact versions, integrity,
and declared licenses. npm installs these packages separately from the
ArmorBreak TGZ. The Windows development ZIP embeds the installed package
directories, with their own license files and notices. The release build
collects those installed texts, any TypeScript ThirdPartyNoticeText.txt,
and the project NOTICE in the release's THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.
